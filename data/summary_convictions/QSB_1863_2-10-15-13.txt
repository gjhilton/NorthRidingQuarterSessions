QSB 1863 2/10/15/13,Summary conviction: William Slowther,17 Feb 1863,Summary conviction of William Slowther for breaking his contract of employment without just cause and without giving notice.  On 16 June 1862 Slowther had contracted with John Hodgson to serve the Mulgrave Ironstone Company as a miner from 16 June 1862 to 30 June 1862 and then from fortnight to fortnight until one party gave noticeOffence committed at the township of Hinderwell on 19 January 1863Case heard at Whitby[The memorandum is dated 17 January 1863 but endorsed as 17 February 1863],https://archivesunlocked.northyorks.gov.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx?src=CalmView.Catalog&id=Q%2fSB%2f1863-Q2%2f10%2f15-13&pos=10187

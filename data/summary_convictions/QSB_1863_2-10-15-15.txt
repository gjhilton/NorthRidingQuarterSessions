QSB 1863 2/10/15/15,Summary conviction: Thomas Green,17 Feb 1863,Summary conviction of Thomas Green for breaking his contract of employment without just cause and without giving notice.  On 28 August 1862 Green had contracted with John Hodgson to serve the Mulgrave Ironstone Company as a miner from 28 August 1862 to 11 September 1862 and then from fortnight to fortnight until one party gave noticeOffence committed at the township of Hinderwell on 19 January 1863Case heard at Whitby,https://archivesunlocked.northyorks.gov.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx?src=CalmView.Catalog&id=Q%2fSB%2f1863-Q2%2f10%2f15-15&pos=10189
